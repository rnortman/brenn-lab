# Known speech

`arctic_a0001.wav` is one utterance of the CMU ARCTIC speech database,
unmodified: speaker `bdl`, a male speaker of US English, saying "Author of the
danger trail, Philip Steels, etc." It is 16 kHz, one channel, 16-bit, 51761
frames (3.2 s), 103566 bytes, sha256
`2594562568c97203d6c3c2c2ff87b9a7c62389100a014934ab0ad39d15af0384`.

It was fetched from
<http://festvox.org/cmu_arctic/cmu_arctic/cmu_us_bdl_arctic/wav/arctic_a0001.wav>.
The database is described at <http://www.festvox.org/cmu_arctic/>. The licence
below is the database's `COPYING`, from
<http://festvox.org/cmu_arctic/cmu_arctic/cmu_us_bdl_arctic/COPYING>, verbatim.

It is the talker the harness's tests play, a source with a real talker's
level and spectrum, marked with `lab mark` before it is played.
`//brenn_lab/testing:speech_test` holds its hash.
