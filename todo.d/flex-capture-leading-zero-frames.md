In two recordings of the reSpeaker Flex on its six-channel image, made on different runs, the first 43 frames of every one of the six channels are 0. It is not known whether the lead is the board's, the USB audio driver's or the recorder's, whether its length is fixed, or whether it is sound that was lost or samples that never existed. It matters to the run's assessment: the assessment measures no stretch of zeros inside a channel, because a rule about one would fire on this lead on a healthy board.

Done when what the lead is has been established, the run-directory reference states it, and the hardware lane's ambient-recording test asserts or prints it as the answer warrants. If the answer is that the recorder or the board's firmware writes it, the entry for that goes to its owner's repository then.
