A dry run of a playback role opens its output without writing a frame, to
show what the device grants. Whether that open makes a sound of its own on the
reSpeaker Flex, an amplifier switching on for one, is not known: the open has
been made, and nobody was at the speaker. Until someone has listened,
`AGENTS.md` has such a dry run made only inside an agreed window. Done when
someone has listened at the speaker while a playback role's dry run opened the
Flex's output and, the open being silent, the sentence in `AGENTS.md` and the
one in the experiments reference say that it plays nothing there. A sound
heard goes to the maintainer before anything is changed.

The Flex's own microphones have recorded the open, in the audible bench's
`test_flex_output_open_is_recorded`, twice: the 500-3500 Hz level of each raw
microphone per half second did not rise in the half seconds the dry run
spanned. In both runs, one to two seconds after the dry run ended, one half
second rose 8 to 13 dB on all four microphones. That is not a listen: the
open may sound below what the microphones pick out of the room, and what
made the later rise, the output's close or the room, is not known.

A play with a stated start holds a real output open with no frame written from
its readying to its start, longer than a dry run's open, under the same quiet
hours as the play.
