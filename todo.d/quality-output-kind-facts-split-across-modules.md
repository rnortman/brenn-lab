What makes a playback output kind is spread over three modules and joined by string comparisons. `OUTPUTS` in `brenn_lab/playback/output.py` maps a kind to the factory that makes its output, and `PLAYS_ON_AFTER_UNDERRUN` beside it says on which kinds frames handed after an underrun are played. `brenn_lab/roles.py` answers the programs a kind needs (`_playback_needs`, the literal `pulse_client`, which the factory `_pulse` also reads) and whether it refuses a sample clock (`_playback_clock_refusal`), each by comparing the kind's name with `pulse`. `brenn_lab/playback/device.py` `read_device` branches on the name for the device keys each kind reads. A kind added to `OUTPUTS` without a matching edit to `_playback_needs` gets no `missing_program` check at configuration and fails with a `KeyError` when its role is made. With two kinds this was chosen over a per-kind record, which nothing then needed. The work, when a third kind is designed: make each `OUTPUTS` value one record per kind carrying its factory, the programs it needs, its clock refusal or none, and whether it plays on after an underrun; `_playback_needs`, `_playback_clock_refusal`, the assessment's reading of `PLAYS_ON_AFTER_UNDERRUN` and playback's `Kind.programs` read that record, and `read_device`'s branch keys on it or is judged to stay. Done when no module outside `output.py` compares an output kind's name to answer one of those facts.
