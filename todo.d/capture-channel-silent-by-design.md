A capture device cannot state that one of its channels may hold nothing but zeros, so the assessment raises `channel_silent` on a channel that is zero by design. The XVF3800's echo reference routed to a capture channel is one: it is zero whenever the device plays nothing, so a run in which the device only records is assessed with a finding though nothing is wrong, and a dependant that requires `clean` has to make its own exception. Done when a capture role's device can name the channels that may be silent, the assessment raises no `channel_silent` on them and says in its channel record that the channel was stated so, a default test shows both, and `docs/reference/run-directory.md` describes it.
