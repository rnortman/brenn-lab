The runner reads the capture kind's own results and events. Its ledger builds the manifest's and the results' capture sections from `Started`, `Stopped`, `Gap`, `Late` and `Failed`, takes `left_running` from the capture kind's `Released`, and takes an unasked stop's counts from `Failed`; the dry run reads `Released` and `Failed` the same way. With one kind that is the whole of what a run records, but a second kind (playback) would add its own arms at each of those places. With the second kind in hand, decide what every kind shares: a seam-level answer to `release` that says whether something was still running, a shape every kind's failure events share, and where a kind's section of the manifest and the results is built. Done when the runner and the dry run test seam types only, and a new kind adds no branch to either. The `TODO(quality-runner-knows-the-capture-kind)` comment sits on the runner's ledger.
