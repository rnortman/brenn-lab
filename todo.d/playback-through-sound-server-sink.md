The playback role plays on an ALSA device it opens directly. An output that exists only behind the desktop's sound server, such as a Bluetooth speaker, cannot be played on. Adding it needs a cap that also covers the sink's own volume, which the role does not control today, and a statement that such an output shares a clock with no capture. A Bluetooth speaker also has to be connected for the work and for its audible tests. The owner's question: may the work connect an already-paired speaker itself, or is connecting it the maintainer's step each time? Until the owner has answered, it is treated as the maintainer's step, and nothing else of the desktop's sound or Bluetooth state is changed. Done when the owner has answered and a playback role configured for a sound-server sink plays on it under its cap, keeps its exact copy, and has an audible-lane test run in an agreed window.
