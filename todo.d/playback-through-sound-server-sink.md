A playback role can be configured for a sink of the desktop's sound server (`output = "pulse"`), and holds the sink's volume and mute under its cap. Every path of it is held in the default check against a stand-in sound server this tree wrote, and read only against the real one in the hardware lane: it has played on no sink. The stand-in and the client were written from one reading of the protocol, so the tests cannot tell a consistent misreading from the truth.

Unmeasured on a real server: that a play sounds on the named sink only; that the stream is made at 0 dB and stays unmoved; that a corked stream leaves a suspended sink silent (so a dry run on a sink is made only inside a window); that a hardware-volume sink reads back at or under what was asked; what the latency figure in a mark's `delay_frames` is worth; what the server converts, and what it does with a channel that has no place on the sink; whether a base volume under full marks the mixer's 0 dB; what the server does with frames handed over after an underrun; what the server sends a stream with no pre-buffer when its buffer plays out at the end of a healthy play, and what offset an underflow message carries then and at an underrun; what the server tells a stream whose sink is suspended while it plays; whether a real sink's latency figure can grow by seconds while a buffer plays out, which would fail a healthy play at the end-of-play bound; and a Bluetooth sink in any respect.

Until the server's end-of-play messages are measured, an underrun as a play's last write is sent is not on the play's record: the play ends `completed` with no underrun and its markers are assessed `expected`, though frames of the last write may not have sounded. Done for that part = such an underrun is on the record and a test holds it.

A Bluetooth speaker has to be connected for the work and for its audible tests. The owner's question: may the work connect an already-paired speaker itself, or is connecting it the maintainer's step each time? Until the owner has answered, it is treated as the maintainer's step, and nothing else of the desktop's sound or Bluetooth state is changed.

Done when the owner has answered, a playback role on a sound-server sink has played under its cap in an agreed window with an audible-lane test that holds each item above, and the reference documents state what was measured.
