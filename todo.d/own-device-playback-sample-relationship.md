A playback role and a capture role that state one `sample_clock` are stated to share a device clock, and a run records, for each play, the frames handed to the device and two marks tying a frame count to the machine's clock and the device's delay reading, and for each capture its start reading. That makes the offset between the kept copy's frames and the capture's frames constant across a run, which the audible-lane test holds, and known from the clocks only to within milliseconds. It does not record what ties a frame of the kept copy to a frame of the capture. Nothing ALSA reports does: the two streams start independently. The tie exists where the device puts its own copy of what it played into the capture, and the reSpeaker Flex's six-channel image has no such channel; whether its chip can be told to route one is part of brenn-pod's `xvf-control-on-usb-audio-image`. Done when a play-and-record run on one device records the relationship between the kept copy's frames and the capture's frames, the reference documents say how exact it is, and the audible-lane test holds it.
