`MODULE.bazel` pins brenn-pod at a revision that is the head of a branch of brenn-pod's public repository and is not on its `main`. The build fetches it by commit id; whether that fetch still succeeds once no branch or tag there holds the commit has not been established, and if it does not, a fresh fetch fails and the gate with it, here and in CI. The work: move the pin, by the steps `AGENTS.md` gives, to a revision reachable from brenn-pod's `main` or from a tag, whose recorder bounds its read of a device's state before a capture starts and ends the read on a stop signal, recording a read cut short as an outcome of its own, and whose `frame-log` has a `write-capture` command that writes a capture frame log from given samples; state the recorder's start timeout against the bound brenn-pod's reference gives; accept the cut-short outcome and carry it into the run's manifest and its assessment, tested; and build the log-writing command from the pin, which is what `alignment-reader-stand-in-in-default-check` waits for. Done when `MODULE.bazel` pins such a revision, the start timeout's comment cites the bound, and a default test shows a capture whose state read was cut short in the manifest.
