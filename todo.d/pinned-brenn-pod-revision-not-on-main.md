`MODULE.bazel` pins brenn-pod at a revision that is the head of a branch on brenn-pod's public remote and is not on its `main`. The build fetches that revision by its commit id. Whether the fetch still succeeds once no branch or tag on the remote holds the commit has not been established; if the branch is deleted, or brenn-pod's `main` takes the work as a different commit, a fresh fetch may fail and the gate with it, here and in CI. The owner's step until then: keep that branch, or tag the commit, on brenn-pod's remote. Done when the pin names a revision reachable from brenn-pod's `main` or from a tag, at which point this entry and its comment are deleted.
